Sourcing & standards

Every bottle in the register is purchased from authorized sellers. We log the purchase, the lot, and the decant date for every vial we pour.

Vials are glass with a metal atomizer cap, chosen because cheap plastic is what actually degrades a fragrance in storage — not the fact that it left its original bottle.
